Evolved phonics

If your child struggles with reading and writing, they aren’t alone. UK Government data tells us that 1 in 5 children don’t meet the expected phonics standard by age six.

Parents are told that ‘more of the same’ will work, but most of those children never catch up. By the end of primary, around 1 in 4 kids don’t meet the expected reading standard.

Thankfully, there’s an evolved approach to phonics that helps struggling readers make better progress. It's called word mapping. The key? We start with the sounds we use to make words, rather than the sound of letters in isolation.
